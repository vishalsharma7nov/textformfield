## 0.1.0

* Field box, text, error and focus colours follow the app's light/dark and festive theme (`context.colors`, `context.brand`).
* `autofillHints` lets the OS fill email, name and one-time codes.
* `errorText` shows a validation message under the field.
* `TextInputAction.next` now moves focus to the next field instead of closing the keyboard.
